What is Required?

  • Sunlight Requirements: Full sun (6+ hours of direct sunlight per day). It can tolerate light partial shade, but flowers most abundantly in full sun.

  • Soil Requirements: Prefers dry to medium, well-drained sandy, rocky, or loamy soils. It performs well in poor, nutrient-deficient soils, but cannot tolerate wet, poorly-drained, or heavy clay soils due to root rot risks.

  • Water Requirements: Low to average water needs. Requires light watering during its first year to establish roots, after which it is highly drought-tolerant.

    • Blooming Season

    Late spring to late summer (typically June through August).
